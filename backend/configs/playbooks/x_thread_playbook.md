# X / Twitter Thread Playbook

- Write a numbered thread of 7–9 posts. Start each line with `1/`, `2/`, etc.
- Post 1: a specific, falsifiable hook (the problem in one concrete sentence).
- Posts 2–3: who has the problem and why current approaches fall short.
- Posts 4–5: what was built and how it works, in plain technical language.
- Post 6: the single strongest piece of evidence actually present in the dossier.
- Post 7: the honest limitation.
- Final post: the ask (feedback, collaborator, or demo link) plus `/p/{slug}`.
- Keep every post under 240 characters. No threads-of-threads, no hype adjectives.

## RULES
- Never invent benchmarks, users, funding, quotes or press mentions.
- If evidence is missing, say what is still unproven instead of filling the gap.
- Do not claim 'viral', 'revolutionary', 'state of the art' or similar.
