# Policy Brief / Short Analysis Playbook

- Open with the exact line: `Analysis, not legal advice.`
- Structure: `Question`, `Why it matters`, `Evidence & sources`, `Recommendation`, `Limitations`.
- Question: one sentence, naming the jurisdiction and the date of the source material.
- Evidence: cite primary sources (statute, regulation, court or consultation) present in the dossier. If none, write `[add primary citation]`.
- Recommendation: one concrete, bounded action. Do not present a hypothetical as an actual case.
- Limitations: state what the analysis does NOT establish.
- Keep the whole brief under 350 words. Neutral, precise tone. No advocacy slogans.

## RULES
- Every legal or statutory claim must carry a citation from the dossier or an explicit placeholder.
- Never include confidential, client or internship matter details.
- Never tell the reader what they should do in a specific legal matter.
