# LinkedIn Viral Hook & Proof-of-Work Playbook

## Strategic Objective
Transform a technical dossier into an irresistible, high-engagement LinkedIn post tailored for software engineers, founders, venture scouts, and academic peers. Cut through corporate fluff with visceral technical clarity and counter-intuitive insights.

## Structure & Formatting Rules

### 1. The Scroll-Stopping Hook (Line 1 - 2)
- Must be under 140 characters before the "...see more" fold.
- Use the **Contrast Pattern**: Contrast a broken industry standard with your empirical proof.
- *Bad*: "I'm excited to announce that I built a new drone project!"
- *Good*: "Centralized drone swarms collapse the second LTE towers go down in a wildfire. So we built a 32-node mesh protocol that coordinates in 12ms over sub-GHz radio with zero GPS."

### 2. The Stakes & Context (Lines 3 - 5)
- Briefly state the core friction or market bottleneck.
- Mention real-world implications (costs, latency, life safety, energy waste).

### 3. The Technical Anatomy (Bullet Points)
- 3 to 4 punchy bullet points detailing the architectural breakthrough:
  - Mention exact frameworks, languages, or protocols (e.g. `Rust`, `pgvector`, `LoRa`, `FastAPI`).
  - Highlight 2 specific quantitative benchmarks (e.g., "99.4% precision", "< 15ms latency", "64% memory reduction").

### 4. Whitespace & Lessons Learned
- What was the hardest failure during prototyping?
- Why did the existing open-source alternatives fall short?

### 5. Call-to-Action & Community Invitation
- Invite targeted feedback or open-source contributors:
  - "Open-sourcing the entire crate today. Check out our Proof-of-Work Dossier and architecture spec in the link below."
  - Ask a high-signal technical question to drive comment engagement: "What consensus protocol would you deploy on sub-GHz mesh under heavy packet loss?"

### 6. Hashtags
- Exactly 3 to 5 targeted niche hashtags (e.g. `#DistributedSystems #RustLang #Robotics #Engineering`).
