# Plain-Language Research Explainer Playbook

- Open with: `Research note — not medical advice.`
- Structure: `What we asked`, `What we did (methods + controls)`, `What we found`, `What we did NOT show`, `Why it matters`.
- Methods must name the control or comparison actually present in the dossier; if none, write `[add control or comparison]`.
- Findings: describe only results present in the dossier. No benefit, cure, safety or diagnostic claims.
- 'What we did NOT show' is mandatory and must be specific.
- End with one link from the dossier (preprint, poster or lab page) or `[add preprint link]`.
- Plain language: define any specialist term on first use. Under 300 words.

## RULES
- Never generate health-benefit, therapeutic or diagnostic claims.
- Do not describe dual-use or biosecurity-sensitive methods in a public explainer.
- Do not imply peer review or clinical validation unless the dossier states it.
