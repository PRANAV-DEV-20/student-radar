# Instagram Reel & Short-Form Video Storyboard Playbook

## Strategic Objective
Distill a complex research or software dossier into a dynamic 30–60 second vertical video storyboard for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Captivate viewers within the first 1.5 seconds and drive them to the bio link or public dossier.

## Video Timing Architecture

### 0:00 - 0:03 | The Visual Pattern Interrupt (The Hook)
- **On-Screen Text**: Bold, high-contrast headline (e.g., "STOP TRAINING AI WRONG" or "32 Drones. 0 Central Servers.").
- **Visual Shot**: Fast cut to a live terminal test, physical breadboard, hardware malfunction, or dynamic architecture visual.
- **Spoken Audio**: "Here is the exact reason why current [X] fails in the real world..."

### 0:03 - 0:15 | The Micro-Problem Demonstration
- Demonstrate the failure of the incumbent solution in 12 seconds.
- Show code trace error, high latency spike, or broken simulator screen.
- Establish urgency: "Every single existing system depends on [Y], which dies the moment [Z] happens."

### 0:15 - 0:35 | The Breakthrough Demo
- Screen recording or camera sweep over the working prototype.
- Call out 2 crisp metrics on screen with animated callout badges:
  - "From 450ms down to 12ms."
  - "Tested on hardware in real-time."
- Show terminal logs or visual dashboard confirming test pass.

### 0:35 - 0:50 | The Secret Sauce
- Explain the single counter-intuitive engineering or design decision in plain English.
- Avoid vague buzzwords; name the specific algorithmic approach or architectural pattern.

### 0:50 - 0:60 | The Outro Loop & Bio Call-to-Action
- Seamlessly transition speech back to the starting phrase so the video loops smoothly.
- **Caption & Action**: "Full architecture spec + interactive dossier published on my Student Radar profile (Link in bio!). Let me know what feature we should build next."
